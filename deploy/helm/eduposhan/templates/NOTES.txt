Watch the rollout (StatefulSets roll one pod at a time):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status statefulset/{{ include "eduposhan.fullname" . }}-api

A failed migration stops the release before any pod changes, and keeps its Job:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/{{ include "eduposhan.fullname" . }}-migrate

Ask a pod why it is not ready:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec {{ include "eduposhan.fullname" . }}-api-0 -- \
    python -c "import urllib.request as u; print(u.urlopen(u.Request('http://localhost:8000/readyz', headers={'Host': '{{ .Values.publicHost }}'})).read())"

Before an exam, raise the floors — autoscaling cannot move sockets that are already open:
  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} deploy/helm/eduposhan -f <your values> -f deploy/helm/eduposhan/values-exam-day.yaml

# ICDS / health translation glossary

Standard translations for domain terms so drafts stay consistent across files
and languages. **Reviewers: correct a term here once and it applies everywhere.**
Acronyms that are widely used as-is (OTP, ICDS, THR, MUAC, LAP) may stay Latin or
be transliterated — note the chosen convention per language.

The Marathi column is a first draft (2026-09), written to match the terms used in
Maharashtra's ICDS system; it goes out for review with the language workbook
(`backend/scripts/export_translation_sheet.py`, "Key terms" sheet). Terms marked
**confirm** are the ones most worth a reviewer's second look.
